Commercial process

Sales-to-Onboarding Process

From commercial commitment to live operation. Clear context, ownership and readiness at every gate.

Aryze commercial flow · 2026

This process begins where the Sales Strategy ends, at commercial commitment. Its purpose is to give Sales, Legal, Compliance, Technical and Onboarding the same context before implementation starts, so the customer experiences continuity rather than a restart.

What changes

Readiness-led handoff.

Sales does not move a customer forward until product path, KYB status, technical scope, blockers and next milestone are clear.

Why it matters

Context is the deliverable.

The receiving team should not rediscover the deal, the use case or the customer's operating constraints.

What it enables

A cleaner start.

Commercial fit, KYB, IT, legal and owners become visible before the handoff. Not after.

Readiness flow.

Six stages from commercial commitment to live. Two decision points. One handoff standard.

Confirm. Document. Verify. Hand off.

Trust work runs in parallel with implementation alignment. Handoff happens when readiness is clear, not when momentum is loud.

Readiness
01

Commercial commitment

Sales confirms fit, intent and a concrete next step.

02

Context captured

Use case, stakeholders, commercial scope documented in the packet.

03

Readiness review

What KYB, legal, technical or operational work is needed.

04

KYB + decision

Trust work starts when fit and intent justify the deeper review.

Implementation requirements, blockers and responsibilities clarified.

Handoff + start

Onboarding starts when scope, owners, blockers and next milestone are clear.

Readiness gates by product.

The packet stays constant. The gates differ.

Pay by Bank

Four-step onboarding.

  • 1. Map payment flow. Volume, markets, current friction, target KPI.
  • 2. KYB. Company verification, documentation, approvals. The gate that can stall everything else.
  • 3. Integration and test. Callbacks, reporting, sandbox journeys, theming.
  • 4. Go live. Monitoring, support, handover to account management.
Stablecoin Factory

Three gates before scoping.

  • Technical readiness. Customer has a technical team able to use sandbox and docs.
  • Issuer model. Customer (or parent/partner) holds the licence. Aryze does not deliver one.
  • Motion fit. Own Programme / Partner-Led / White-Label confirmed before commercial scoping.
Digital Cash

Three institutional gates.

  • Licence. Institution already licensed for digital cash or related activity.
  • Executive sponsor. Board-adjacent champion identified before deal progression.
  • Multi-stakeholder capacity. Treasury, legal, compliance, risk and tech can engage in the same room.

A single review meeting is enough when context is clear. Two meetings (commercial + implementation) when KYB, technical or legal questions need a forum. More than two means the deal is not ready for handoff.

Time targets.

Internal expectations, not customer promises. A slip past target without a documented reason escalates.

Pay by Bank

6–12 weeks SMB.

  • Commitment → KYB started: 5 working days.
  • KYB started → approved: 1–3 weeks (customer-doc dependent).
  • KYB approved → integration started: 5 working days.
  • Integration → live merchant: 2–6 weeks.
Stablecoin Factory

Weeks to a quarter.

  • Commitment → sandbox-to-test-net: 2 weeks.
  • Sandbox → first deployment: 2–6 weeks.
  • Own Programme cycle: weeks to a quarter.
  • Partner-Led / White-Label: one to two quarters / two quarters+.
Digital Cash

6–18 months.

  • Commitment → first workshop: 3 weeks.
  • Workshop → architecture: 4–6 weeks.
  • Architecture → legal / risk / procurement: 2–6 months.
  • Phase-1 deployment: 4–6 months on narrow scope.

Escalation paths.

Escalation is part of the process. A stall becomes harder to resolve the longer it sits.

KYB stalled

Commercial + Compliance

Triggered when KYB docs are incomplete after 2 weeks. Joint review within 5 working days.

Technical stalled

Technical lead + Commercial

Triggered when integration questions remain open after the implementation review. Joint review within 5 working days.

Legal stalled

Legal + Executive sponsor

Triggered when contract review extends beyond target time, or when indemnification, IP or exit becomes deal-critical. For Digital Cash, the executive sponsor engages personally.

Customer quiet

Commercial re-engagement

10+ working days of silence triggers a structured re-engagement attempt before flagging the deal at-risk.

Scope creep

Commercial leadership

Material scope beyond original commitment routes to commercial leadership before anyone agrees. Especially relevant for Digital Cash multi-year framework agreements.

Aryze capability gap

Product + Commercial leadership

An Aryze-side blocker (RWA, sandbox access, undocumented limit) routes to product immediately. Never promise the capability to the customer in the meantime.

How we measure handoff quality.

The process is judged on whether onboarding starts with context, not whether it starts fast.

Conversion

  • Commitment → readiness review
  • Readiness review → KYB started
  • KYB → onboarding-ready
  • Onboarding-ready → implementation start

Speed

  • Commitment to readiness review
  • Readiness review to KYB start
  • Implementation review to handoff
  • Time stuck with unresolved blockers

Quality

  • Product path documented
  • Stakeholders identified before handoff
  • KYB and legal blockers known
  • Onboarding starts without re-discovery

The decision question: does each handoff give onboarding enough context, ownership and readiness to move without avoidable rework?

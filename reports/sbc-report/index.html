What the trip showed.

Three observations that should drive follow-up, not generic summaries.

Finding 1

The market validated the positioning.

Conversations confirmed demand for lower per-transaction cost, faster settlement and fewer chargebacks. The Pay by Bank pitch resonates with payment-driven iGaming operators.

Finding 2

The value is in distribution, not direct sales.

Apcopay, GameBridge.io, Finnplay, BridgerPay and SoftConstruct should be treated as access points to multiple operators, not single-customer leads.

Finding 3

Relationships need ownership and cadence.

Line Ho Young Peteri, Jesper, Anne, Angela and Steve should be managed as strategic network relationships with a named owner and a simple cadence, not ad-hoc contacts.

Risks and open points.

Control points before Aryze commits technical capacity.

Decision power

Mandate not always clear.

Several Tier 2 contacts have roles to confirm. Clarify mandate and decision power in the first follow-up call before scoping.

Integration

Tech discovery after commercial qualification.

Do not commit technical capacity until commercial fit is confirmed. Avoid sandbox or API work without a named decision owner.

Compliance

Bring compliance in early.

iGaming leads need MLRO and KYB review in parallel with commercial discovery, not after pilot is agreed.

Market coverage

Prioritise where Aryze delivers.

UK live today. EU/EEA late Q2 2026 via Payabl and Volt. Do not promise coverage outside that footprint.

Follow-up speed

Momentum cools fast.

The relationships from SBC weaken if the next contact slips past day 7. The 0–48 hour window is the single biggest leverage point.

Network ownership

Person-dependent introductions.

Jesper, Anne, Angela and Steve introductions risk being ad-hoc. One named owner per relationship before introductions are pursued.

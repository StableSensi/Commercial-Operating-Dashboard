Event overview

Use the booth to create dwell time and qualify while visitors wait.

The target audience is iGaming operators, crypto exchanges, fintech and payment decision-makers. The presence should make Aryze visible, approachable and easy to remember after the conference.

Conference Nordic Blockchain Conference Stockholm.

Branded booth with interactive activities and live product demos.

Position Payment infrastructure with three product routes.

Pay by Bank, Stablecoin Factory and Digital Cash should all be visible.

Outcome Qualified leads, not passive visitors.

Lead capture, qualification and follow-up are part of the booth design.

Booth system

Visible energy, clear product story and simple conversion.

Display

Brand and infrastructure message

Two roll-up banners, booth logo placement and QR code for lead capture.

Print

One-pagers for each route

Pay by Bank, Stablecoin Factory, Digital Cash and a general Aryze overview.

Activity

Mini Golf: The Payment Race

One obstacle lane shows payment friction; the clear lane shows “The Aryze Way”.

Demo

Pay by Bank and FX Swap

Live demo running at the booth as the conversation starter.

Lead capture

Capture, classify and follow up before momentum cools.

Visitors sign up through an Aryze-branded tablet page before playing the booth activity. Submissions go to the NBC Stockholm contact list in Brevo and trigger post-event follow-up.

Fields Full name, company, position and email.

Enough data to qualify fit, identify role and personalise follow-up.

Lead status Hot, warm or cold.

Hot leads book a call before leaving; warm leads are flagged; cold leads enter newsletter.

Follow-up flow 48 hours, day 3 and day 7.

Personal note, product deep-dive and soft CTA for demo call or webinar.

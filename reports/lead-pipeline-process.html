Commercial process | Lead pipeline | 2026

How to read and use the lead pipeline.

The pipeline shows where each lead sits, who owns it, which product path it belongs to and what should happen next. The board should be used for status and movement, not long written explanations.

Pipeline rule Status, owner and next step must be visible.

If those three things are unclear, the lead card is not ready for review.

Pipeline board

Read the board from left to right.

Each column is a stage. A lead card moves right when the lead becomes more concrete. Side stages keep long-term, parked or closed leads out of the active sales flow.

Lead card

The lead card is the quick status view.

A lead card should be readable without opening it. It should show who the lead is, who owns it internally, which company it belongs to, the product path, any agent or referral, and the next task.

Lead card fields

What should be visible

  • Contact is the person or lead name at the top of the card.
  • Owner is the internal Aryze person responsible for follow-up.
  • Company shows the organisation behind the lead.
  • Product shows the likely path: Pay by Bank, Digital Cash, Stablecoin Factory or another relevant route.
  • Agent captures the referral or introduction source when relevant.
  • Task is the next concrete action, such as follow-up, meeting booking or waiting for response.

Inside a lead card

Use Notes, Tasks and Files for the detail behind the card.

When a lead card is opened, the tabs give the working context. The board should stay clean; details belong inside the lead.

Notes Use notes for status and history.

Write what happened, what was sent, what the lead answered and what should happen next.

Tasks Use tasks for the next action.

Create tasks for follow-up, booking, sending material, checking with an agent or waiting for response.

Files Use files for material shared or received.

Attach one-pagers, decks, agreements, screenshots or any document needed to understand the lead.

Working rule

Update the board when the status changes.

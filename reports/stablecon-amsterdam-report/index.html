What the trip showed.

Three observations that should drive the next two weeks.

Finding 1

Visa is the single most important commercial signal.

Visa indicated they can help close the payout gap on the Pay by Bank side. Pay by Bank today is PIS-only and customer-to-merchant only, with no standalone payout flow. Visa Direct push payments would close the gap and let us put a complete inbound-plus-payout proposition to merchants without changing capability boundaries.

Finding 2

BVNK and Visa share one risk: drift.

Both put the design work on us. Visa said "tell us what you want from the relationship". BVNK offered rails access without naming the next step. The next step sits with Aryze on both. That is the right place for it to sit and the place where deals die from inaction.

Finding 3

The C-level dinner is a working channel.

The Utila / Aleo / Sumsub dinner produced two warm contacts at Utila (Paz van Gelder, Shahar Friedman) and concrete follow-up. Paz accepted an introducer arrangement built around his gaming-sector network — a direct iGaming pipeline lead into Pay by Bank's primary ICP, with follow-up agreed in Stockholm.

Risks and open points.

What management should attend to before the next two weeks close.

Risk 1

BVNK and Visa drift.

Both put the next step on Aryze. The Visa conversation in particular is the single most important commercial signal from the trip because it closes the Pay by Bank payout gap. Treat as P0.

Risk 2

Mastercard positioning.

The wallet-plus-card angle invites Aryze to act like a wallet or issuer. Capability discipline applies: Aryze does not issue, does not hold the licence, does not operate. Position as infrastructure into the stack.

Risk 3

Quantoz posture confusion.

Mixing tooling-supplier and peer-issuer postures in one conversation is the surest way to walk out with nothing. Decide before the next call. Default: tooling supplier.

Risk 4

Four-people, four-versions drift.

Rabii, Jodi, Bertram and Jeppe will remember different things from BVNK / Visa / Mastercard. Run the internal debrief inside seven days and align on the partner narrative we put in front of customers.

Risk 5

Dinner detail fading.

Memory of the C-level dinner cools fast. Rabii to capture remaining conversation themes and commitments to this report within three days.

Risk 6

Capability over-promise.

Visa, BVNK and Mastercard conversations all tempt expansion claims. Stick to canonical capability boundaries when presenting Aryze across the trip outputs.

Marketing plan | Pay by Bank | April-May 2026

Build trust first. Convert second.

The Pay by Bank campaign educates high-risk merchants on the true cost of card processing and positions Aryze as a faster, cheaper and more reliable account-to-account payment infrastructure partner.

Primary outcome Qualified inbound demand

Demo bookings, free setup sign-ups and warmer commercial conversations.

Open source file
Duration 6 weeks Weeks 16-21
Audience High-risk iGaming, crypto, marketplaces
Offer 50 free setup places
Core channels 4 SEO, LinkedIn, email, event

Campaign logic

Lead generation through education and credibility.

High-risk merchants often pay materially more for card processing through fees, failed payments, reserves, chargebacks and FX friction. The campaign should make that cost visible, then present Pay by Bank as the direct, regulated and operationally simpler alternative.

ICP iGaming operators, crypto exchanges and online marketplaces.

Decision-makers with payment pain, complex settlement needs and high cost sensitivity.

Positioning Danish-built payment infrastructure for serious merchants.

Use regulatory seriousness, network reliability and operational clarity as trust anchors.

Conversion Move from education to demo or setup conversation.

Every asset should create a next step: demo, webinar, roundtable or free setup sign-up.

Channel system

Each channel has a specific job.

SEO / Blog

Own the education layer

Publish explainers around Pay by Bank, card-cost pain and high-risk merchant payment flows.

LinkedIn

Build visible authority

Use organic posts, sponsored reach and founder/team content to reach ICP decision-makers.

Brevo

Nurture and convert

Use newsletter and email flows to move readers toward demo calls, event attendance and setup.

Events

Create qualified conversations

Use roundtables, conference anchors and themed moments such as Bitcoin Pizza Day.

Campaign calendar

Six-week execution rhythm.

Wk 16 Launch education base

Publish “What is Pay by Bank?” and launch announcement posts.

Wk 17 Explainer and newsletter

Carry core blog into short-form video, LinkedIn and Brevo newsletter.

Wk 18 Deepen authority

Publish the complete guide and connect content to demo and setup CTAs.

Wk 19 Event and roundtable push

Drive attendance and direct conversations with ICP decision-makers.

Wk 20 Video and recap assets

Use explainer video, social recap and newsletter to compress learning into action.

Wk 21 Wrap-up and conversion

Package campaign assets, push demo CTA and identify the strongest commercial leads.

Budget and dependencies

Keep spend focused on reach, moments and conversion assets.

Most production can be handled in-house. Paid spend should stay targeted: LinkedIn reach for ICPs, roundtable execution, free setup conversion and conference/event anchors.

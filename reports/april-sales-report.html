Current State

April pipeline position and stage maturity.

April activity separated into a Pay by Bank merchant track and a Stablecoin lead and opportunity pool. The numbers below are a monthly operating view, not a long-term strategy.

Route Current position What April showed April learning
Pay by Bank 19 merchant leads currently in pipeline. The strongest April fit was UK payment-driven merchants in gaming, betting, trading and higher-risk or card-under-served segments where settlement speed, chargebacks, deposits, top-ups and reconciliation matter commercially. The lead base is commercially relevant, but April did not yet create a clean signed/live conversion readout.
Stablecoin Factory Stablecoin Factory sat inside the 12 lead and opportunity Stablecoin pool and was most relevant for customers seeking simple deployment rather than full institutional infrastructure. The strongest April fit was builders, technical teams, innovation teams, product teams and exploratory operators that want a faster AI-native, self-serve route from concept to simple live deployment. Factory demand was visible, but April evidence still needed clearer separation between curiosity and real deployment fit.
Digital Cash Digital Cash also sat inside the 12 lead and opportunity Stablecoin pool, with relevance strongest among credible licensed-institution tracks. The strongest April fit was already-licensed institutions that need settlement, treasury, ramps, multi-currency issuance, interoperability or asset movement under their own licence and governance. Digital Cash interest was more institutional and slower moving, with maturity depending on licence position, governance and operational readiness.

Product-Level Readout

What April showed by product.

April activity did not read as one single pipeline. Pay by Bank produced the clearest lead base, while Stablecoin Factory and Digital Cash required more qualification inside the Stablecoin pool.

April Pay by Bank signal

Pay by Bank

Pay by Bank showed its strongest fit where payment pain was already close to margin and operations. The buyer was not looking for more payment-method variety. The buyer wants better payment performance.

Best-fit customers
UK payment-driven merchants in gaming, betting, trading and higher-risk or card-under-served verticals.
What the buyer wants
Faster settlement, lower friction, cleaner reconciliation and stronger control.
April result
19 merchant leads created the clearest commercial base for near-term sales review.

April Factory signal

Stablecoin Factory

Stablecoin Factory appeared strongest in narrow, private and qualification-led conversations. It is an AI-native self-serve route for teams that need a practical first step, not a full institutional operating model from day one.

Best-fit customers
Builders, technical teams, innovation teams, product teams and exploratory operators with a clear use case.
What the buyer wants
Simple deployment, less friction, faster learning and a credible upgrade path.
April result
Stablecoin Factory demand appeared inside the Stablecoin pool, but required sharper use-case qualification.

April Digital Cash signal

Digital Cash

Digital Cash read as serious infrastructure for already-licensed institutions. The buyer did not need a token demo. The buyer needs a credible operating layer behind issuance.

Best-fit customers
Already-licensed institutions with concrete settlement, treasury, liquidity or value-movement needs.
What the buyer wants
Operational depth, institutional control and a route from issuance to real operation.
April result
Digital Cash interest was more institutional and depended on formal credibility, governance and licence context.
April communication lesson

Pay by Bank was easier to communicate openly in April. Stablecoin Factory and Digital Cash needed more private, context-led follow-up.

April Learnings

What April taught the commercial team.

Observed lessons

Ownership matters

The strongest opportunities had product fit, owner, next step and date.

Progress beats activity

Meeting volume was less useful than movement into discovery, demo, scoping, onboarding or formal process.

Product separation is necessary

Pay by Bank, Stablecoin Factory and Digital Cash produced different signals and cannot be read as one pipeline.

Stage maturity was uneven

The Stablecoin pool had activity, but April showed that screening and qualification were still critical.

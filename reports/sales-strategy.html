Sales Strategy

How Aryze turns market activity into qualified opportunities, clear ownership and next steps.

Aryze commercial strategy · Sales operating model · 2026

Aryze's sales strategy is built around sharper qualification, product-specific sales motions and clear ownership from first signal to committed next step. The goal is simple: focus on the accounts where Aryze has the strongest right to win and move them through a visible pipeline.

Focus

Prioritise the accounts where Aryze has a clear right to win.

Sales effort should concentrate on segments where payment economics, compliance, settlement, issuance or deployment speed create a real commercial reason to engage.

Method

Use product-specific motions instead of one generic pitch.

Pay by Bank, Stablecoin Factory and Digital Cash each require a different buyer conversation, proof point and decision path.

Control

Move every serious opportunity through owned next steps.

Progress should be visible through qualification, decision points, stakeholder alignment, risk checks and documented ownership.

The shift is from activity to qualified movement.

The sales team should create demand, qualify it quickly and move only credible opportunities into structured commercial work. This gives management a clear view of where momentum is real and where attention should stop.

Avoid

Broad selling with unclear ownership

Generic pitchUnqualified interestLoose follow-upLate risk reviewUnclear owner

This creates activity, but it does not create a reliable view of conversion, risk or management priority.

Agent and relationship-led origination model

Agents, memberships and relationships can help Aryze reach the right people faster. They are useful when they create trusted access, clear context and a realistic next step.

Agents as a focused origination layer

Agents can widen access to relevant buyers and reduce cold-start friction. They should not be treated as generic lead sources.

  • Company and buyer context
  • Relevant product path
  • Problem or use case
  • Route to decision-maker
  • Recommended next step
Standard: A warm introduction matters only if it creates qualified commercial movement.

Memberships and associations

Memberships should support ecosystem access, partner visibility and relationship-led openings across priority markets.

Standard: Membership value should be judged by qualified conversations, not visibility alone.
Agent quality test

Useful introductions answer four questions.

Why this account?Why this product?Who owns it?By when?

If the team cannot answer those questions, the introduction is still unqualified activity.

Commercial role

Relationships should improve access quality.

Agents, partners, events and memberships should create more relevant conversations, stronger buyer context and faster movement into the right product motion.

The commercial operating flow

A simple map for moving serious prospects from first signal to qualified opportunity, while keeping room for focused follow-up where the customer needs more clarity.

Six stages. Two decision points. One progression standard.

The flow is intentionally simple: identify the signal, qualify fit, prove relevance, align stakeholders and move forward only when ownership and the next decision are clear.

One operating model. Three sales motions.

The strategy stays consistent, but the conversation changes by product. Each product has its own buyer, value story, complexity level and progression path.

Market focus should follow where Aryze has a clear right to win.

The strategy should prioritise segments where Aryze can create measurable commercial value, not simply broad market awareness.

Pay by Bank priority markets

Focus on merchants where payment performance has direct commercial impact.

  • Gaming and betting operators
  • Trading and high-frequency deposit environments
  • Card-under-served or higher-risk merchant categories
  • Businesses with settlement, chargeback or reconciliation pressure
Strategic intent: Turn current merchant interest into a smaller set of owned, high-fit opportunities.

Stablecoin priority markets

Keep Stablecoin Factory and Digital Cash separated by buyer maturity and implementation depth.

  • Builders and product teams for Stablecoin Factory
  • Innovation teams seeking controlled pilots
  • Licensed institutions for Digital Cash
  • Partners with treasury, settlement, issuance or infrastructure needs
Strategic intent: Separate exploratory demand from serious institutional opportunities.

Pipeline strategy is about selection, ownership and movement.

The pipeline should not be managed as a list of names. It should show where Aryze has credible fit, who owns the next step and which opportunities deserve management attention.

01
Prioritise

Fit before volume

Lead volume matters only when the customer problem, buyer maturity and product path are credible.

Management ruleReview quality of fit before celebrating activity.
02
Segment

One product path per opportunity

Every serious opportunity should be routed to the product motion that best matches the customer problem.

  • Pay by Bank
  • Stablecoin Factory
  • Digital Cash
  • Partner or ecosystem path
Management ruleMixed messaging weakens progression and accountability.
03
Own

Named owner and next date

A serious opportunity needs one accountable owner, one next action and one date for review.

Management ruleNo owner means no managed pipeline.
04
Progress

Movement over optimism

Progress should be visible through stage movement, customer commitment or a documented decision.

  • Discovery completed
  • Demo completed
  • Scoping started
  • Commercial next step agreed
Management ruleIf an opportunity does not move, it should be reset, nurtured or closed.

How management should judge the strategy.

The strategy should be measured by the quality of movement, not the volume of activity. The right scorecard makes weak pipeline visible early.

Pipeline quality

  • Product path assigned
  • Buyer problem clear
  • Owner and next date visible
  • Stage reason documented

Commercial movement

  • Qualified meetings created
  • Demos completed
  • Scoping conversations started
  • Low-fit opportunities removed

Market signal

  • Repeatable Pay by Bank merchant pain
  • Factory demand with clear use cases
  • Digital Cash interest from licensed institutions
  • Agent introductions with real buyer context

Decision question

Are we concentrating commercial time on the opportunities where Aryze has the strongest right to win?
